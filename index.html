I’m a final-year Computer Science (Honours) student at Ontario Tech University, finishing my courses in April 2027. I’m looking for full-time software engineering or AI engineering roles starting May 2027. I’m based in Oshawa, Ontario, and I speak English, French, and German fluently.

I’m now writing my honours thesis with Dr. Ken Pu, testing whether speech models pick up on sentiment. I also co-organize HackApro, a bilingual youth hackathon with 60+ participants.
